          How to play

          • Attacking throws one die fewer than the province holds. The die that stays home doesn't fight, so a 5-stack attacks with 4 — while the defender throws their whole stack. That is why attacking an equal stack is a losing bet.
          • Highest total wins, and ties go to the defender. Win and you take the province and its dice; lose and your stack collapses to 1.
          • End turn pays one die for every province you own, placed where you need it most.
          • Alliances are public, and they are not a truce. Click a player in the rail to propose a pact, and answer the offers made to you — the whole table sees who asked whom. An ally stops counting as a threat when your dice are placed, so a friendly border stops draining your reinforcement. Attack one anyway and the pact breaks on the spot: that is legal, and sometimes the right move.
          • Eight dice is the cap on any one province.
          • The last player with land wins. Neutral land never attacks, but it grows — the longer you take, the harder it gets.
